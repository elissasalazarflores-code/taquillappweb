01 Who we are

The comprehensive orchestrator of healthcare projects

BKM is a group of healthcare professionals and companies specialized in the health sector, covering our clients' needs in medical equipment, medical devices, supplies and eHealth solutions.

We connect capital, operational knowledge and supply chain to transform opportunities into executed health projects, delivering with operational excellence, commitment and patient impact, and enabling business conditions that create a prosperous ecosystem for every stakeholder.

We are not distributors. We are not consultants.
We are the vehicle that makes health projects happen.

Mission

We are the strategic partner that integrates intelligent financing, specialized operation and supply chain to deliver healthcare projects end to end, with predictability and control.

Vision

To become the most relevant healthcare execution platform in Latin America, with an ecosystem that generates real impact, recurring revenue and sustainable value for all participants.

02 The problem we solve

Mexico's healthcare market is complex. We make it executable.

Fragmented access

A complex institutional system with multiple public and private actors operating without integration.

Financial risk

Unpredictable collections and prolonged payment cycles that affect cash flow.

Regulatory processes

Certifications, permits and approvals that require in-depth local expertise.

After-sales support

Lack of technical follow-up, maintenance and clinical support after installation.

Inconsistent distribution

Traditional channels lacking technical capabilities and effective territorial coverage.
